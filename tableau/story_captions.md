# Tableau story captions

**Story point 1.** Self-published ISBNs rose 38.7% in 2025.

*Annotation:* Self-published ISBNs rose 38.7% to 3.53 million in 2025, while Circana BookScan reports print unit sales up only 0.3% to 762.4 million. Supply is outgrowing demand, so margin per sale matters more each year.

**Story point 2.** KDP nets $6.79 per paperback; IngramSpark nets $3.33.

*Annotation:* The gray channel share is the largest deduction on every wholesale route. On IngramSpark, the 55% bookstore discount takes $9.34 of each $16.99 before printing.

**Story point 3.** Retailer discounts drive most of the cost variance.

*Annotation:* IngramSpark's $3.47 unfavorable variance to KDP breaks down into $2.55 of channel share, $0.60 of print cost and $0.32 of market access fee. The channel discount, not printing, is the main driver.

**Story point 4.** Direct ebook uploads keep the most per sale.

*Annotation:* Direct stores pay $3.49 on a $4.99 ebook. Aggregators trade $0.35 (Draft2Digital) to $0.52 (IngramSpark) per unit for one-upload reach.

**Story point 5.** KDP breaks even at 158 unit pairs; IngramSpark at 272.

*Annotation:* KDP recovers $1,585 of Year-1 fixed costs after 158 unit pairs; IngramSpark needs 272. Platform fees alone are recovered within 42 pairs on every route, so the real breakeven risk sits in production spend.

**Story point 6.** Subscriptions win only above 16.6 units a month.

*Annotation:* KDP leads in the bear, base and bull cases. PublishDrive moves ahead of IngramSpark once volume passes the 16.6-unit monthly crossover, which happens in the base and bull cases.

**Story point 7.** Cash arrives two to three months after each sale.

*Annotation:* At month 12, $948 of royalties has been earned but not yet received. IngramSpark pays about 60 days later than B&N Press, which raises days sales outstanding and the working-capital need.

**Story point 8.** Kindle Unlimited payouts vary month to month.

*Annotation:* The per-page rate averaged $4.55 per 1,000 pages with a 6.1% coefficient of variation, ranging from $4.19 to $5.01. Pool-based income works like music-streaming royalties and should be forecast as a range.

**Story point 9.** A $2 price cut needs a 21.4% sales lift to pay off.

*Annotation:* A $16.99 to $14.99 price cut needs a 21.4% unit lift just to break even, which takes about 18,467 visitors per arm to detect at a 2% conversion rate. Set the breakeven lift before the test starts.
