# Change log

Each quarterly refresh records what changed in prices, fees, rates and market figures.

## October 14, 2026
- Tableau story restyled to match the slide deck: navy page, dark panel cards for text, white cards for charts, orange section kickers, serif headings and a blue story navigator. Deck and story both moved to a slightly brighter navy. No rates changed.
- Tableau story: reordered the cards to follow a real launch (market, KPIs, money kept per sale, cost variance, ebooks, breakeven, scenarios, profit calculator, getting paid, Kindle Unlimited, price testing, recommendations, sources), now 15 points. The deck's "Where each number comes from" slide and the README use the same numbering. No rates changed.
- Tableau story: new point 10, an interactive profit calculator with sliders for books sold and launch budget, a route picker, four scorecards, a donut of costs earned back, smiley faces for every route, a profit curve with your books-sold marker and a ranked leaderboard. No rates changed.
- Tableau story: platform and cost colors are now fixed in the workbook (they no longer depend on legend order); sentence case in every title; plain-language axis titles on every chart; scenario panels use their own scales so the bars fill the width; Kindle Unlimited chart has labeled points and call-outs for the peak, the low, the January dip and the latest month; A/B test shading and call-out no longer overlap. No rates changed.

## October 12, 2026
- Added plain-language notes to every chart, two-chart slide pages, an introduction to key concepts, and a decision-tree walkthrough for each stakeholder type.
- Tableau story: plain-language notes, numbered clickable source names, and a final "what should you do" story point. No rates changed.
- Tableau story: added an introduction point covering purpose, scope and stakeholders, renamed the closing pages to "Recommendations" and merged Paths A to E onto one page, and set the story as the default view on Tableau Public. No rates changed.
- Charts and Tableau story: added markers, shaded ranges and in-graph callouts that point at each key insight (for example the 70% ebook ceiling, the 158 to 511 pair breakeven range, the $948 month-12 cash gap, the 45 to 105 day payout range and the 21% to 27% price-test bar). Every highlight is computed from the data files, so it updates with each refresh. No rates changed.
- Tableau story: numbered the story cards 1 to 11; moved callouts so none overlap; expanded the Kindle Unlimited line chart (axis no longer starts at zero, shown in dollars per 1,000 pages with high, average and low lines); simplified busy charts (gross-to-net labels only what the creator keeps, cost variance labels only the store's cut, the breakeven page drops the six-line curve, scenario panels read slow, normal, great in order, A/B (version A against version B) test names shortened); added a chart type, analyst reading and plain-language reading to every page; spelled out every acronym with a short meaning, including a key terms section on the introduction; made the story tall enough that pages no longer scroll inside it. No rates changed.
- Explained where every number comes from: a new analysis/methods.py computes 14 KPI (key performance indicator) definitions with formulas and worked examples, plus the calculation behind each story page, from the data files. It feeds a new How the numbers work section in the README, three new deck slides, two KPI pages and a Where the numbers come from page in the Tableau story (now 14 points), and a How it is calculated line on every analysis page. No rates changed.
- Tableau story: renamed the chart guide labels to "Technical/analytic audience" and "In plain terms (non-technical audiences)". No rates changed.
